The sheet as the Inventory

Decided on 22 September 2026: A1, with the filigree in the header, the motes and the per-section tint. It's already on the site; this page stays as a record of the comparison. The bench ghost was tried on the site, with two different images, and removed because it looked ugly: its switch no longer paints anything.

The header and the sheet, remade as the game's Inventory screen: black, filigree, the whole HUD, the nail large and lit, the spells and arts as plates on black and the charms in their band. The figures are the real ones: the mockup loads the engine, and the masks and the charm grid respond to taps.

A1 · One page stacks everything. A2 · Two pages puts Inventory and Charms side by side, like the game's two pause tabs, and on mobile it stacks just like A1. Equip something to see how the numbers move; the dashed line in the wide frame is the fold of a 1440 × 900 screen.

What goes into the site

  • The <style> block marked → tokens.css and the one marked → app.css, almost as they are. The @container vp queries belong to the mockup; on the site they're the @media ones already there.
  • hudHtml(o, prev): the HUD comes out of knightSideHtml as a pure function and the arena and the sheet both use it. On the sheet, the masks are still the health control.
  • The flash comes from E.diff in commit(): only what just changed glows, and it doesn't glow again on every repaint.
  • Nothing goes behind a figure: the bench ghost, only behind Equipped. The figures stay in --font-num and the accent, only on what's touched.

Today's reference

  • At 1440 × 900 and without the guide, the first row of figures is at y ≈ 460, the masks at y ≈ 800 and the spells at y ≈ 975, below the fold. The sheet is ~1200 px tall.
