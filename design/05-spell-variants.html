Spell impacts

Five ways of saying how many of a spell's impacts land, over the same four cases: Shade Soul against Broken Vessel (it can hit it twice), Descending Dark (three parts and the side of the 1st burst), Abyss Shriek (four equal bursts) and Shade Soul with Flukenest (sixteen flukes).

They all work: touch the controls and the button's damage changes. The numbers are the site's; so are the CSS, the tokens and the sprites.
