## 2. Nail Arts

**Strength doesn't affect them.** Fury does. A single rounding.
Charge: **1.35 s**, or **0.75 s** with Nailmaster's Glory (1 notch; the best cost/benefit ratio in
the game for art builds).

| Art | Multiplier | 0/1/2/3/4 |
|---|---|---|
| Great Slash | ×2.5 | 12 / 22 / 32 / 42 / **52** |
| Dash Slash | ×2.5 | 12 / 22 / 32 / 42 / **52** |
| Great Slash / Dash Slash + Fury | ×4.375 | 22 / 39 / 57 / 74 / **92** |
| Cyclone Slash | ×1.25 **per hit**, 3–6 hits | 6 / 11 / 16 / 21 / **26** per hit |
| Cyclone + Fury | ×2.1875 per hit | 11 / 20 / 28 / 37 / **46** per hit |

Cyclone Slash gives **3 hits** as standard and up to **6** if you mash ATTACK; you can move
horizontally during the spin. It's the art that deals the most total damage (up to 156 with the
Pure Nail) and the best minion clearer.

  Three Shrieks with Shaman Stone kill Uumuu; three kill Flukemarm.
- **Descending Dark** is the defensive tool: i-frames during the whole fall.

## 6. DPS: how to think about it

```
nail DPS = damage / cooldown
```
Cooldown **0.41 s**, or **~0.28 s** with Quick Slash (−32%):

- Pure Nail alone: 21 / 0.41 ≈ **51/s**
- + Quick Slash: 21 / 0.28 ≈ **75/s**
- + Quick Slash + Strength: 32 / 0.28 ≈ **114/s**
- + Fury (at 1 mask): 56 / 0.28 ≈ **200/s** ← the theoretical ceiling

That's why *Quick Slash* (3 notches) is the strongest sustained-damage charm in the game, ahead of
Strength: it's a **×1.47** that multiplies **all** your output, soul generation included (11 soul
every 0.28 s instead of every 0.41 s), while Strength is a ×1.5 that only touches the nail's
damage and not the arts.

The 0.28 s comes from `max(cooldown, swing duration)`: with Quick Slash the cooldown drops to
0.25 s but the hitbox still lasts 0.28 s, so the duration rules.
`js/engine.js` already computes it this way, and the site shows these same figures.

The flip side: striking faster = more recoil and pushing the enemy more. With light bosses,
pair it with **Steady Body**, or strike **upwards** (the upward swing doesn't push horizontally).
