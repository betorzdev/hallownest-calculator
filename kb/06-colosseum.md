# Colosseum of Fools and the Eternal Ordeal

It's at the very top of Kingdom's Edge. **There are no checkpoints inside a Trial**: if you die,
you start the whole Trial again. Your Shade appears in the warriors' pit, to the west, not in the
arena.

| Trial | Entry | Requirement | 1st-time reward | Later rewards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **of the Warrior** | 100 geo | — | Charm Notch + 1000–1024 geo | 1000–1024 geo |
| **of the Conqueror** | 450 geo | Warrior | **Pale Ore** + 2000–2020 geo | 2000–2020 geo |
| **of the Fool** | 800 geo | Conqueror | 3000–3020 geo | 3000–3020 geo |

Behind the Colosseum, by breaking a wall east of the pit, is the **Pale Lurker**, who drops a
**Simple Key**.

---

## 1. Target priorities (for all three Trials)

1. **Primal Aspid** (35 health) — it fires three blobs in a 35° fan with the middle one aimed at
   you. **Always kill it first.** Great Slash, Dash Slash or Shade Soul with Shaman Stone kill it
   in one hit.
2. **Mantis Petra** and **Soul Twister** — very dangerous in the air, but they fall to a single
   Great Slash.
3. **Armoured Squit** (40) — they come in groups and strike at once. **Cyclone Slash** protects
   you on both sides and sweeps them.
4. **Shielded Fool** — slow and melee only: **leave it for the end** of each wave and use it to
   refill soul and heal.

## 2. Charms for the Colosseum

- **Nailmaster's Glory** (1 notch): charges the arts in 0.75 s. Recommended even in spell builds.
- **Shaman Stone**: Shade Soul one-shots Primal Aspids and Squits.
  In the floorless section soul can't be spent on healing anyway.
- **Mark of Pride / Longnail**: reach for downward strikes from a platform.
- **Dream Wielder**: loads of soul. Leave a slow enemy alive at the end of the wave and farm it
  until you're fully healed.
- **Weaversong** (+ *Grubsong*): makes the Trial of the Fool's final boss **trivial** — stick to
  a wall with the Super Dash and let the weaverlings kill the Tamer and the Beast. And it
  generates soul with every hit.
- **Hiveblood**: it heals you on its own during the floorless section.
- **Grubsong + Thorns of Agony**: in a long fight you're going to take hits; at least let them
  give you soul and deal damage.
- **Dreamshield**: blocks projectiles and gives extra reach from platforms and walls.

## 6. The Eternal Ordeal

A secret challenge in the Hall of Gods. **It's unlocked by default**, you don't need to have met Zote.

**How to get there**: at the east end of the Hall of Gods' second level, break the ceiling above
the **third arch from the right** (Howling Wraiths find it and break it well, but the nail works
too). Go up to the hidden ledge with the Super Dash from the right wall (*Sharp Shadow* also
reaches) and a double jump to grab on. Inside, break the left wall that rumbles: Zote's statue
comes in.

**Goal**: kill as many Zotelings as possible before dying. At **57** the counter turns gold and
unlocks the *The Eternal Ordeal* menu style (a title screen with Zote singing the main theme).

Rules:
- It starts with **Zoteling the Mighty** (200 the first time, 120 afterwards; **he does deal
  contact damage**, unlike the Colosseum's Zote).
- A new Zoteling only appears when one dies, chosen at random among the available types.
  **Never more than 2 of the same type at once.**
- **They all speed up with each death.** It's subtle, it's very noticeable around 50 kills;
  around **80**, the charging ones cross the arena in ~1 s.
- The Dream Nail can only be used on the starting Zoteling and on the winged, hopping and
  volatile ones.

| Zoteling | Behaviour | Health | Kills to appear |
|---|---|---|---|
| the Mighty | Like the Colosseum's Zote, but **damages on contact** | 200 / 120 | 0 |
| Winged | Flies towards you, turns slowly | 60 | 1 |
| Hopping | Approaches in high, slow jumps | 60 | 1 |
| *(shockwave jumper)* | Jumps from side to side; **on landing it creates a shockwave** that covers part of the arena | 190 | 8 |
| *(hanging)* | Hangs from the ceiling and spits acid; **no contact damage**; leaves by itself (doesn't count) | 100 | 15 |
| *(lanky)* | Like the Mighty but with a taller hitbox and more needle reach | 150 | 20 |
| *(crusher)* | Falls from the ceiling with a **shockwave covering the whole arena** and stays still for a while; **gives no soul**; leaves by itself | 200 | 25 |
| Volatile | Appears in the air and **explodes** on dying or after a few seconds: **2 masks** | 30 | 30 |
| *(rising)* | Comes out of the floor and rises leaving acid blobs; leaves on reaching the ceiling | 100 | 35 |
| *(soul-sucker)* | Floats and **drains your soul**; no contact damage | 100 | 40 |
