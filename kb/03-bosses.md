# Boss entries

**About the names**: bosses and attacks go in English, which is how the wiki indexes them and
how the community names them; the Spanish name follows in italics where it has been checked
(for a boss, the official one; for an attack, the fan name the site uses in `js/enemies.js`
comes from the Spanish wiki). Charm, nail and spell names are the game's official English ones.

**Health**: `base` = a normal playthrough. `At` = Attuned (Godhome). `AsRa` = Ascended and
Radiant (same health, the damage changes). Five values separated by `/` = health by nail level
0/1/2/3/4 (some bosses scale with you). Pantheons: PotM = of the Master, PotA = of the Artist,
PotS = of the Sage, PotK = of the Knight, PoH = of Hallownest.

**Damage**: in masks. When not stated = 1 mask.

# Main route

## Gruz Mother · *Madre Gruz*
**Health** 90 (140 in the Colosseum) · **At** 650 · **AsRa** 945 · Pantheon of the Master, room 2

- **Charge** (*Embestida*): a short charge towards your position, then it bounces.
- **Wild Slam** (*Golpeteo salvaje*): it crashes between floor and ceiling, advancing little by little.

On dying in the Forgotten Crossroads it releases 7–8 Gruzzers that also have to be killed (not in
Godhome). With the Mothwing Cloak the Wild Slam is trivial. 6 Vengeful Spirits or 18 Old Nail
hits kill her. On Ascended the floor becomes 3 platforms with 4 spike pits: that's the hard
part, not her.

## Vengefly King · *Rey vengamosca*
**Health** 55 (100 Colosseum) · **At** 450 · **AsRa** 735 (left) / 430 (right) · PotM room 1

- **Swoop** (*Picado*): opens its jaw and comes down in a tight "U".
- **Summoning Scream** (*Grito invocador*): calls 2 Vengeflies (75% each). Greenpath: up to 10
  times. Colosseum: never. Godhome: up to 15, at most 5 alive.

Jump the swoop and strike downwards as it passes. Kill the flies at once: they're easy soul.
There's plenty of room to heal right after dodging a swoop.
**On Ascended/Radiant and in the Pantheon of Hallownest there are TWO**, one on each side.

## Brooding Mawlek · *Mawlek Incubador*
**Health** 300 · **At/AsRa** 1050 (Pantheon of Hallownest: **750**) · PotM room 10

- **Slash** (*Zarpazo*): only if you get close. It can chain it with the spit.
- **Spit** (*Escupitajo*): 3–4 blobs in an arc towards your side. Then it leaps or vomits.
- **Leap** (*Salto*): it lands where you were and bounces back to its spot.
- **Vomit** (*Vómito*): lots and lots of blobs at once; it stays still until they've all fallen.

Longnail and Mark of Pride avoid the slash's retaliation. During the Vomit you can dash over it
before the acid falls. The combo *Baldur Shell + Cyclone Slash* lets you chip at it from the edge
and heal inside its body without taking damage.
On Ascended the middle platform disappears.

## False Knight · *Falso Caballero*
**Health** 355 total = 65×3 (armour) + 40×4 (maggot) · **At** 260×3 · **AsRa** 560×3 · PotM room 3
**Damage** the direct slam deals **2 masks**; shockwave and barrels, 1.

- **Leap** (*Salto*; stops using it in phase 3) · **Charge** (*Carga*; ends in a Leaping Bludgeon).
- **Slam** (*Mazazo*): leans back, strikes the floor, a shockwave that crosses the arena. In
  phase 2 barrels fall; in phase 3, more.
- **Leaping Bludgeon** (*Mazazo saltando*): leaps and comes down where you were.
- **Rage** (*Furia*): after being staggered and after you hit the maggot, it jumps to the centre
  and pounds on both sides for 3 s, throwing barrels. If you leave the maggot unhit for too
  long, it gets up without going into Rage.

Keys:
- The armour **gives no soul**. Only the exposed maggot does. Save your soul or wear *Grubsong*.
- The **barrels can be redirected with the nail**: 10 damage each, and they can stagger it.
  It's the best source of damage during the Rage.
- After the first Rage a piece of wall over the left door breaks: you can run away, get
  Vengeful Spirit in the next room and come back.
- Three staggers and a final Rage: it breaks the floor and falls; you go down and finish off the maggot.

## Hornet Protector · *Protectora Hornet*
**Health** 225 · **At** 900 · **AsRa** 1250 · PotM room 5 · **Stagger** 11 hits (6 in a combo, <2 s)

- **Lunge** (*Estocada*) · **Aerial Lunge** (*Estocada aérea*; if it hits a wall she clings to it for a moment).
- **Thread Storm** (*Tormenta de hilo*): an area around her, also in the air.
- **Needle Throw** (*Lanzamiento de aguja*): she throws it and pulls it back; **the return also deals damage**.

At the start she takes a combat stance: **1 second still and vulnerable**, strike upwards
non-stop. After each stagger **she always jumps**: a guaranteed opening for 2–3 hits. Heal:
during the Thread Storm and the Needle Throw. If you miss the jump over the needle, a downward
strike bounces off it without damage.

## Mantis Lords · *Señores mantis*
**Health** 210 (phase 1) + 160+160 (phase 2) = **530** · **At** 400 + 350×2 · **AsRa** 500 + 600×2 · Pantheon of the Artist room 5

Three attacks, always preceded by a teleport:
- **Lance Dash** (*Embestida de lanza*): she lands on one side and crosses the whole arena at floor level.
- **Lance Drop** (*Caída de lanza*): she appears above you for an instant and comes down vertically.
- **Blade Boomerang** (*Bumerán de hoja*): she clings to the wall and throws the blade. If she's
  **low**, the arc goes up; if she's **high**, the arc goes down. It comes back.

Phase 2 (two at once): they can dash simultaneously from both sides, and the boomerangs are
synchronised in a **wide arc** (they meet in the centre at floor level) or a **short arc** (they
hang in the centre and come down).

- The less health they have, the faster they attack.
- **The boomerang is the healing window**. With phase 2's short arc, staying **in the centre of
  the arena** the blades don't come down far enough: you heal 2 masks with no charms.
- Stay on one side: the far one tends towards the boomerang and the near one towards melee.
  Avoid the corners (the teleport for the dash hits you).
- The boomerangs can be **pogoed** and *Dreamshield* destroys them.
- *Shape of Unn* lets you duck under the Lance Dash.
- There's a lifeblood cocoon in the north-west corner behind a breakable wall.

## Soul Warrior · *Guerrero del alma*
**Health** 180 (Sanctum) / 300 (the Elegant Key room and Colosseum) · **At** 750 · **AsRa** 1000 · PotM room 9

- **Dive-Slash** (*Picado con tajo*): teleports above you and comes down slashing. **It always opens with this.**
- **Dash-Slash** (*Embestida con tajo*) · **Conjure Orb** (*Orbe perseguidor*; the same as Soul Master's).
- **Summon** (*Invocación*): only the Elegant Key version and the Colosseum's first one spawn
  Follies. On Ascended in Godhome it summons up to **36 Follies**.

The openings are right after the dive (you step aside) and after the dash (you jump over it and
strike downwards).

## Soul Master · *Maestro de Almas*
**Health** 275 + 110 = **385** · **At** 600 + 350 · **AsRa** 900 + 600 · PotA room 3 · **Stagger** 9 (7 in a combo, <1 s)

Phase 1 — it teleports between each attack:
- Side **Dash** (*Embestida*) · a homing **Orb** (*Orbe*) until it hits.
- **Clock** (*Reloj*): it crosses the arena with 4 orbs spinning around it; then it disappears and
  **the orbs come back on their own** like a boomerang.
- **Slam** (*Impacto*): it falls vertically and sends out shockwaves on both sides (its Desolate Dive).
- **Fake Out Slam** (*Impacto falso*): it starts the fall, resets halfway and falls again on your
  new position.

Phase 2 (after the fake death, it breaks the floor):
- **Altered Slams** (*Impactos alterados*) chained 4–7 times, with a wide explosion instead of shockwaves.
- **Altered Orb** (*Orbes alterados*): it floats for 8.5–10.5 s conjuring 3–4 orbs from random
  points. That's when it's completely open.

The easiest: stick right under it and strike upwards while it conjures orbs. The Orb **can't reach
you if you're pressed against it**. In the Clock, get close when it's covered half the arena and
jump the lower orb. During its "deflating" there's an instant when you can damage it **without
knocking it out of the stagger**. Phase 2 is easier but barely lets you heal.

## Dung Defender · *Defensor del Estiércol*
**Health** 700/750/800/850/900 (it grows with your nail) · **At** 800 · **AsRa** 1100 · PotM room 8
**Stagger** 16 hits — **or just 1** with Desolate Dive / Descending Dark

- **Dung Toss** (*Lanzamiento*): a ball; it bounces until it touches the floor 3 times. Then he dives in.
- **Burst** (*Erupción*): he buries himself for 3 s and comes out firing 4 balls (2 per side) that **don't bounce**.
- **Toss Combo** (*Combo de lanzamiento*): two balls in a row, and then either **he rolls up** and
  bounces with them (5 bounces) or **erupts** (5 s underground).
- **Dive** (*Buceo*): in and out of the floor 2–3 times towards the centre.
- **Zeal** (*Fervor*; at 50% health, only once): ~12 s chaining eruptions every half second.
- **Evade** (*Evasión*): if you get close, he rolls backwards.
The trick that decides the fight: **a Desolate Dive while he's underground brings him out and
staggers him**. Use it to cancel the Zeal completely. His balls don't break with the nail but
**they do with any spell**. Pressed against the left wall you're almost safe: he always tries
to emerge near you and the balls bounce off the wall.

## Crystal Guardian · *Guardián de cristal*
**Health** 280 · **At** 650 · **AsRa** 900 · PotA room 2

- **Laser Beam** (*Rayo*): aims with its arm and fires where you were. Dangerous for ~0.5 s.
- **Sky Beams** (*Rayos del cielo*): it shouts and up to 4 beams fall from the ceiling, at ±15°
  angles, each at a different moment. **It always opens the fight with this.**
- **Hop** (*Salto*), short or long.

All the beams are telegraphed by a thin thread of light. Its armour **prevents pogoing** and
being on top of it is dangerous. It can be killed from outside the arena without triggering the fight.

## Enraged Guardian · *Guardian Furioso*
**Health** 450/450/500/550/600 · **At** 650 · **AsRa** 1250 · Pantheon of the Knight room 1
**Damage 2 masks**

The same three attacks, more aggressive, and the Sky Beams fall **one after another** instead of
at once (now orange, not pink). In the easternmost section of the room most of the sky beams
don't reach you: you only have to watch the arm beam. When it hops close it tends to fire
backwards → several free hits.
With *Kingsoul* it can be killed with Shade Soul from outside the arena.

## Watcher Knights · *Caballeros Vigía*
**Health** 6 × 220/220/220/240/260 = **1320–1560** · **At** 350 each · **AsRa** 600 each · PotK room 9

- **Double Slash** (*Tajo doble*): horizontal and then downward vertical, advancing.
- **Roll** (*Rodada*): a ball that crosses the arena. **Immune to the nail, not to spells.**
- **Bouncing Roll** (*Rodada saltarina*): two bounces seeking you; the first reaches the ceiling.
- **Back Roll** (*Rodada atrás*): only when there are two active.

There are 6, but **never more than 2 at once**. One starts; after 20 s (when the music rises)
the second comes in. If you kill the first one fast, **two come in together**.
- Kill one **before the fight** with the chandelier in the previous room (in Godhome the number
  can't be reduced).
- Their only answer to the air is the ball jump → attack them from above.
- 3 Descending Darks with Shaman Stone kill one; the i-frames cover you.
- *Dream Wielder* lets you draw soul from them while they roar (they're immune to damage then,
  but not to the Dream Nail).
- *Sharp Shadow* + Shade Cloak damages them **even while rolling**; with *Dashmaster*, more.

## Uumuu
**Health** 300 · **At** 350 · **AsRa** 700 · Pantheon of the Sage room 9

- **Burst** (*Descarga*): fills the arena with sparks in one of two patterns. The bottom
  platforms are **always** safe; at the top, either the left or the right. It lasts 3.5 s.
- **Chase** (*Persecución*): creates a discharge on you every 0.5 s for ~4 s (8 in total, each
  lingering ~2 s). You have to keep moving.

**It can't be damaged** until Quirrel bursts its membrane: he shows up after you dodge 3–4
attacks the first time and 2–3 the following ones; it stays open for **3.75 s**.
- 3 Abyss Shrieks with Shaman Stone kill it **in a single cycle** if you arrive with full soul.
  It's the standard way.
- **In Godhome there's no Quirrel**: Uumuu summons **Oomas**. You have to make an Ooma's explosive
  core hit it: it breaks the membrane **and deals 50–150 damage to it**. It's immune to the Oomas
  while it screeches to summon more. On Ascended a harmful green mist appears at the bottom of the arena.

## Nosk
**Health** 680 · **At** 680 · **AsRa** 980 · PotA room 8

- **Charge** (*Carga*): a short screech and it crosses the whole arena without stopping even if it touches you.
- **Leap** (*Salto*) · **Eruption** (*Erupción*; 2 blobs at its feet + 8 on each side; always followed by a Charge).
- **Rain** (*Lluvia*; only below 560 health): it goes into the ceiling for ~6.5 s dropping blobs.

**The two safe spots**: pressed against the left or right wall of the central platform. From
there the Charge passes over you and almost everything else misses; you can heal with no
charms. The only thing it doesn't cover is the Rain — there you have to move.
During the Rain **its hitbox is still on the ceiling**: Grimmchild, Glowing Womb, Longnail/Mark
of Pride and Howling Wraiths reach it.
The screech before the Charge is the tell; it tends to charge to the centre before an Eruption.
On Ascended the central platform disappears (goodbye safe spot).

## Broken Vessel · *Receptáculo Roto*
**Health** 525 · **At** 700 · **AsRa** 1000 · PotA room 10 · **Stagger** 13 (9 in a combo, <1 s)

- **Slash** (*Tajo*) and **Aerial Slash** (*Tajo aéreo*): a dash covering ~75% of the arena, slashing mid-run.
- **Backstep** (*Paso atrás*) · **Leap** (*Salto*).
- **Flail** (*Molinete*): 4 slashes in an arc over its head.
- **Slam** (*Machaque*): jumps and lands on you; the impact releases **4 blobs** in an arc.
- **Cascade** (*Cascada*): it moves to the centre and releases a tide of blobs in waves of 3
  every 0.4 s for ~4 s. Only **3 times in the whole fight**: at 370, 220 and 110 health.
- **Balloon** (*Globo*): spawns an Infected Balloon (with **1** health, not 15) every 4–5 s, in
  parallel with everything else, at most 3 alive. It starts below 420 health.

It takes **recoil** → Vengeful Spirit usually hits twice (even more so with Shaman Stone).
During the Cascade, Desolate Dive/Descending Dark: a lot of damage and zero risk. *Cyclone
Slash* corners it and staggers it easily. *Spore Shroom* and *Defender's Crest* clear the
balloons while you heal.

## The Collector · *Coleccionista*
**Health** 750/750/750/800/850 · **At** 900 · **AsRa** 1200 · PotS room 3 · **Stagger** 14 (11 in a combo, <2 s)

- **Jar** (*Tarros*): it jumps to the ceiling and jars rain down. As they fall **they deal
  contact damage without breaking**; on touching the floor they release a Vengefly, a Baldur or
  an Aspid Hunter. Phase 1: 1–2 jars, at most 5 creatures. Phase 2 (after 350 damage): 2–3
  jars, at most 6. With 4 or more creatures alive **it stops throwing jars**.
- **Grab** (*Agarre*): it jumps towards you, flailing. Surprisingly long range.
- **Hop** (*Saltito*): its way of moving.

- **It gives no soul** except with the Dream Nail or with weaverlings + *Grubsong*.
- Aim: the Coiled Nail to kill the creatures in one hit (or the Channelled Nail + Strength).
- *Cyclone Slash* clears the creatures and hits it at the same time.
- Stay **on the ground**; you fit underneath when it jumps.
- In the normal game, on reaching 0 health it stays laughing: it takes **15 more hits** to
  finish it off (it deals no contact damage in that state).
- On Ascended it summons Primal Aspids, Sharp Baldurs and Armoured Squits (26 health) and only
  1 jar at a time in phase 1.

## Hornet Sentinel · *Centinela Hornet*
**Health** 700 · **At** 800 · **AsRa** 1200 · PotS room 10 · **Stagger** 13 (7 in a combo, <2 s)

Everything from the Protector but much faster and with a wider Thread Storm, plus:
- **Parry** (*Parada*): she puts her needle on guard. If you hit it, she laughs and answers with
  a wide slash.
- **Spike Traps** (*Trampas de púas*; below 480 health): 2–3 hanging knots of blades. They're
  destroyed with the nail, spells, Grimmchild or Dreamshield. At most 7 at once.

She always **jumps on coming out of the stagger**. Shade Soul is excellent: it damages, pushes
her far out of the Lunge and clears traps. The traps can be pogoed. *Grimmchild* prioritises
the traps over her. Tank strategy: *Quick Slash + Lifeblood Heart + Grubsong + Stalwart Shell*,
soak hits and heal at every stagger.

## Traitor Lord · *Señor desleal*
**Health** 800 · **At** 800 · **AsRa** 1300 · PotK room 4
**Damage 2 masks** on everything except the Dancing Glaive (1) and contact (1)

- **Dive** (*Picado*): jumps vertically and comes down diagonally at 45°. **Both parts deal damage.**
- **Dash** (*Embestida*): runs and claws, a third of the arena.
- **Dancing Glaive** (*Guadaña danzante*): two orange blades in cosine waves that move apart;
  they last ~3 s.
- **Ground Pound** (*Machaque de suelo*; below 500 health): **floor-to-ceiling** waves faster
  than you, crossing the whole arena.

It's huge and the arena is small. Keys:
- **The Glaive and the Ground Pound only come out if you move away.** Staying close it only
  dashes and dives.
- The Ground Pound **can only be avoided with invulnerability**: Shade Cloak or Descending Dark.
- To dodge the Dive, **move in the direction of the attack**: it passes over you.
- Its fight entrance animation already deals 2 masks.
- If you followed Cloth's quest, she shows up and hits it for **15 per club blow**.
- *Sharp Shadow* is the cheese: all its physical attacks seek you, so you pass through them.

## The Hollow Knight · *Hollow Knight*
**Health** 250 + 350 + 400 = **1000**, **+250** if you reach phase 4 → 1250 · **Stagger** 13 (9 in a combo, <1 s)
It doesn't appear in Godhome (Pure Vessel replaces it)

Phase 1 (at 1000):
- **Triple Slash** (*Tajo triple*): three sweeps advancing; **they're so wide they hit you from behind**.
- **Lunge** (*Estocada*): covers ~50% of the arena.
- **Parry** (*Parada*): 0.75 s. If you hit it after the needle glows, it takes no damage and
  counters by sliding with a huge arc.

Phase 2 (below 750): adds
- **Diving Flame Pillar** (*Pilar de llamas en picado*): it jumps or teleports and falls
  vertically; columns of infected fire come out. **2 masks.**
- **Barrage** (*Descarga*): 9–10 blobs in fixed arcs from bottom to top, ~75% of the arena. **It
  doesn't follow you: the arcs are always the same.**

Phase 3 (below 400): it loses the Lunge and the Triple Slash (except right after teleporting)
and **it can no longer be staggered**. Adds
- **Outbreak** (*Brote*): it floats in the centre for ~6.5 s spitting blobs along three routes
  (left, right, down).
- **Bouncing Balloon** (*Globo rebotante*): it floats and drops 2–5 times seeking you.
- **Self Stab** (*Autoapuñalamiento*): it stabs itself with its needle. **While it lasts, all
  the damage you deal to it is reduced to 1**, whatever your nail.

Phase 4 (after reaching 0, it heals 250): only Bouncing Balloon, Self Stab and
**Deterioration** (*Deterioro*; it attempts the Triple Slash, swings once and collapses).

- **Its weakness is the air**: during the Lunge and the Triple Slash, jump and pogo on its head.
- Against the Parry: the safe thing is **not to attack**; if you trigger it, pass through the
  counter with the Shade Cloak.
- During the Barrage and the Outbreak **walk**, don't dash: the dash overshoots the gaps.
- With *Void Heart*, at the start of phase 4 you can hit it with the **Dream Nail** → a fight
  against the Radiance.
- The Dream Nail is no use against it outside of that; *Flukenest* only works during the Flame
  Pillar; the area effects (*Spore Shroom*, *Defender's Crest*) don't touch it in its Parry stance.

## The Radiance · *El Destello*
**Health** 350 + 400 + 250 + 700 = **1700** (by phases)
**Damage 2 masks on everything**, except the **floor spikes** (1) and falling into the Void in phase 4 (1).
**It deals no contact damage.** Its wings and legs **can't be damaged**.

Stagger by damage, at two fixed points: the phase 2→3 transition (at 1000 accumulated damage)
and the final blow (at 1700).

Attacks:
- **Beam Burst** (*Ráfaga de rayos*): 8 beams at 45°, three bursts in a row rotated at random.
  Dangerous for ~0.5 s, only at their widest point. **A beam never falls twice in a row in the
  same place**, so burst 1 and burst 3 can coincide.
- **Sword Burst** (*Ráfaga de espadas*): 12 swords that form one by one around its face and go
  out at once, curving gently.
- **Sword Rain** (*Lluvia de espadas*): groups of 12 swords spread over 18 columns; there are
  always gaps and even the space between two adjacent swords is enough to fit. Phases 1–2: 4
  groups. Phase 3: **endless**.
- **Sword Wall** (*Muro de espadas*): 6 swords in 9 rows crossing the arena; there's always a gap.
  4 walls in phases 1–2, 2 in phase 4. Each wall takes ~3 s to cross.
- **Wall of Light** (*Muro de luz*): sweeps the arena in ~3.5 s. Only the wide band deals damage;
  the lines in front and behind don't. **It can only be dodged with the Shade Cloak** (or
  Descending Dark). Variant: the wall disappears in the centre and another comes out from the
  opposite side.
- **Orb** (*Orbes*): up to 3 homing ones, one every 1.65 s; they die on hitting something or
  after 2.75 s.
- **Spike Floor** (*Suelo de pinchos*): phase 2, half the floor, 5 s; phase 3, permanent.
  **1 mask**. If they touch you they disappear for a while (in phase 3 they come back almost at once).
- **Big Beam** (*Gran rayo*): only phase 5, one every 1.5 s, more and more accurate.

Phases:
1. Open arena, everything except spikes and Big Beam. It ends at 350 damage.
2. Adds the spike floor. It ends at 400.
3. It stays still in the centre; permanent spikes on the sides; **only endless Sword Rain**
   until 250 damage.
4. An arena of small platforms over the Void. Everything except Rain, spikes and Big Beam.
   700 damage. **It's the only phase in which it can be hit with the Dream Nail.**
5. An ascent jumping platforms with the Void rising behind, dodging Big Beams. On reaching the
   top, **a nail hit** (no other source works) closes the fight.

Builds that work:
- **Resources**: *Quick Focus + Deep Focus* (you heal 2 masks in almost normal time) +
  *Soul Catcher* / *Grubsong*.
- **Elegy**: in phases 1–3 the projectiles from *Grubberfly's Elegy* reach it **without
  jumping**; with *Quick Slash*, very strong. And it boosts *Grubsong*.
- **Spells**: it has a **huge hitbox and doesn't recoil** → all 4 impacts of *Abyss Shriek*
  always land. With *Shaman Stone*, 120 per cast.
- **Lifeblood**: *Joni's Blessing* or *Lifeblood Heart/Core* if you can't manage to heal.
- The orbs can be **baited into the floor** to get them off you; passing through one with the
  Shade Cloak destroys it.

# Optional and area bosses

## Massive Moss Charger · *Musgoagresor gigante*
**Health** 100 · **At** 480 · **AsRa** 850 · PotM room 4 · **You aren't locked in**, you can leave.

Charge and belly-flop. Hitting it does **not** expose the bug inside (unlike normal Moss
Chargers). At the edge of the room it gets stuck for an instant when doing the belly-flop. With
*Quick Slash* and/or *Heavy Blow* it dies just from hitting it during the charge.

## Flukemarm · *Tremarmita*
**Health** 350 · **At** 500 · **AsRa** 900 · PotA room 9 · **You aren't locked in.**

A single attack: **it spits 2 Flukefeys** from its orifices (telegraphed by infection coming
out), at most 6 alive.

- You need to kill the Flukefeys **in one hit**: the Channelled Nail, or the Sharpened Nail + Strength.
- 3 Abyss Shrieks with Shaman Stone kill it.
- Continuous pogoing on its head, killing Flukefeys on the way up, works with no charms at all
  (Longnail/Mark of Pride make it much easier).
- **Never fall into the water**: swimming, you can't attack.
- On Ascended the first 6 Flukefeys have more health.

## Oblobbles
**Health** 260 ×2; when one dies, the other goes up to **300** and heals up to 100 → 560–620 total
**At** 450 ×2 + up to 650 · **AsRa** 750 ×2 + up to 750 · PotA room 4 · Trial of the Conqueror

- **Acid Cannonade** (*Cañonada ácida*): 6 volleys of 4 shots at 90°, each volley rotated 30–45°.
- **Fly** (*Vuelo*) diagonally, bouncing. If you hit it from behind, it turns.
- **Frenzy** (*Frenesí*): when one dies, the other speeds up a lot and goes to **8 volleys** of 4.
  In Godhome it goes up to **650** health and heals 200.

The optimum is to **spread the damage** so the survivor goes into Frenzy with little health
(spells spread it on their own). 2–4 Abyss Shrieks kill one. In Frenzy the Nail Arts are better
than single swings because of the short time between cannonades. The corners are the safe
place to heal.

## Hive Knight · *Caballero Colmena*
**Health** 800/800/800/850/920 · **At** 850 · **AsRa** 1300 · PotS room 1 · **Stagger** 17 (12 in a combo, <1.5 s)

- A low **Lunge** (*Arremetida*), ~65% of the arena.
- A somersault **Leap** (*Salto*) up to the ceiling; **it doesn't chase you**.
- **Swarm Release** (*Liberación del enjambre*; below 549 health): it spits 12 Hivelings to the
  ceiling; **7** come down in arcs following you. Meanwhile, it lunges, leaps and does a third
  attack. The fight **always starts** with 7 Hivelings falling without it on screen.
- **Surprise Slash** (*Tajo sorpresa*): it teleports next to you (either side) and slashes ~35%
  of the arena. **It also does it if you're in the air.**
- **Honey Spikes** (*Púas de miel*; below 779 health): 3 orbs with 8 spikes each in the upper
  two thirds; they never share a row or a column. They burst **in the order they formed**,
  every half second. They can be **pushed with the nail** but not destroyed.

It's a **light** boss: it gets pushed a lot. That's why *Steady Body* is huge here, and with
*Quick Slash* it's best to strike **only upwards** so you don't chase it. *Mark of Pride* keeps
you out of its reach. *Heavy Blow* helps against the Hivelings.
The Shade Cloak passes through the Surprise Slash and the Lunge. *Cyclone Slash* is brutal
because you can move while spinning.
In the Pantheon of Hallownest **it starts directly in phase 3**.

## Winged Nosk · *Nosk alado*

## God Tamer · *Domador de Dioses*

## Marmu — 150 Essence
**Health** 200/320/416/500/570 · **At** 416 · **AsRa** 600 · PotA room 7

## Galien — 200 Essence
**Health** 230/368/479/570/640 · **At** 650 · **AsRa** 1000 · PotS room 7

## Markoth — 250 Essence

# Dream Bosses

They're accessed with the Dream Nail on the original boss's body. You don't die on losing
either → *Fragile Strength* and *Fragile Heart* are free.

## Failed Champion · *Campeón Fallido* — 300 Essence
**Health** 360×3 (armour) + 60 + 40×3 (maggot) = **1260** · **At** 360×3 · **AsRa** 600×3 · PotK room 7
**Damage**: contact and physical hits, **2 masks**; shockwaves and barrels, 1.

The same as False Knight but much faster, and with **the same order of phases**: armour, maggot
(**60** the first one), armour, maggot (40), armour, maggot (40) and, after the final Rage, he
breaks the floor and falls: last maggot (40).
- **Leap** (*Salto*): only from phase 2 → the Leaping Bludgeon is much more frequent.
- **Slam** (*Mazazo*): faster, bigger and taller shockwave. **Barrels fall in every phase.**
- **Leaping Bludgeon** (*Mazazo saltando*): a lower jump. It also throws barrels in every phase.

The big trick: **you can extend the stagger as long as you like by hitting the armour** (it
restarts the timer without damaging the maggot) → Dream Nail + healing to the full. With *Dream
Wielder* + *Quick Focus* you recover all your health every stagger. Careful: close the stagger
**by hurting the maggot**; if it gets up on its own that phase doesn't count.

Others: he gives no soul → kill Maggots near his body beforehand and draw soul from them with the
Dream Nail (it doesn't work in Godhome), or *Weaversong + Grubsong*. Dodge **under** his jumps
(better with the Shade Cloak + *Sharp Shadow*). The barrels are sometimes blocked by
*Dreamshield*. Cheese: a wall + a charged Super Dash out of his reach, with *Grimmchild /
Weaversong / Glowing Womb* doing the work.

## Lost Kin · *Familiar Perdido* — 400 Essence

## White Defender · *Defensor Blanco* — 300 Essence (1st victory)

- **Zeal** (*Fervor*; once, at 600 health): ~11 s chaining **6 Bursts** every 1.5 s.

## Grey Prince Zote · *Príncipe Gris Zote* — 300 Essence (1st victory)

# Godhome: exclusive bosses

## Brothers Oro & Mato · *Hermanos Oro y Mato* — the Pantheon of the Master's finale
**Health** Oro 500 (phase 1) + Oro 600 and Mato 1000 (phase 2) = **2100**
**At** Oro 500/600 + Mato 1000 · **AsRa** Oro 800/1000 + Mato 1000 = **2800** (the wiki, in "Hall of Gods" and in his page's `AsRa_Health`; the ~1600 that was here had no source)

Phase 1 (Oro only):
- **Double Slash** (*Tajo doble*): a backhand + a high slash. **Its reach is very deceptive.**
- **Jump Slash** (*Tajo saltando*): he jumps to your position, stops in the air and comes down
  slashing. **He picks the destination before taking off** → you can hit him as he sets off.
- **Dash Slash** (*Corte con embestida*): his art. He jumps off screen, lands at one end,
  **charges for ~1.5 s** and crosses 75% of the arena (the slash covers the rest).
- **Roll Back** (*Rodar atrás*) / **Jump Back** (*Saltar atrás*).

Phase 2 (Mato comes in, with **Cyclone Slash** instead of the Dash Slash — he goes up high
spinning for ~1.5 s moving towards you and comes down like a Jump Slash):
- **Alternating Strikes** (*Golpes alternos*): if you're on one side, they take turns; if they
  chain 4–6 without you interrupting them, one releases his art. **Getting between the two
  interrupts the sequence.**
- **Twin Combo Attack** (*Combo gemelo*): if you're in the middle, they both attack at once,
  facing each other.
- **Barricade** (*Barricada*): one puts his needle on guard while the other does his art.
  **Hitting him there doesn't damage him** and he answers with a Double Slash or a Jump Slash.

If you kill one, the other carries on; the brother who's sitting down can have soul drawn from
him with the Dream Nail. **Mato recovers 1 second before Oro** after attacking: a shorter
punishing window. Often one stays still while the other attacks → free damage.
Healing: *Shape of Unn + Quick Focus* lets you heal through everything except the Dash Slash.
For the Jump Slash, **dash towards him**, not away: his front foot lands where you were.
They can be pogoed while they charge their arts.

## Paintmaster Sheo · *Maestro de Pinturas Sheo* — the Pantheon of the Artist's finale
**Health** 950 · **At** 950 · **AsRa** 1450 · **Stagger** 12 (9 in a combo, <1 s)

**Each attack is announced by the colour of the brush**:
- **Magenta → Great Slash** (*Gran corte*): he charges with a white aura, strikes the floor
  creating a column of paint, and then drops fall all over the arena.
- **Yellow → Paint Spear** (*Lanza de pintura*): he draws the brush back and throws a javelin

- **Red → Paint Stomp** (*Pisotón*): he jumps, freezes up high, sweeps creating **6 drops in an

- **Blue → Paint Splash** (*Salpicadura*): 3 drops in an arc at different distances.
- **Roll** (*Rodada*; 40% of the arena) and **Leap** (*Salto*) to reposition.

He doesn't stay still: if you get close, he rolls; if he's in a corner, he jumps out before

## Great Nailsage Sly · *Gran Sabio del Aguijón Sly* — the Pantheon of the Sage's finale
**Health** 800 (phase 1) + 250 (phase 2) = **1050** · **At** 800/250 · **AsRa** 1200/600
**Stagger** 15 (11 in a combo, <1.5 s) · His needle has the reach of **Longnail + Mark of Pride
together**.

Phase 1:
- **Slash Combo** (*Combo de tajos*): two slashes 0.5 s apart (a high downward one + an upward
  one). If you're still close, **he finishes** with a 45° jump spinning like a wheel of blades
  for ~0.75 s.
- **Nail Slam** (*Machaque de aguijón*): he jumps, falling short, does a somersault and drives
  in where you were; he usually chains an upward slash.
- **Great Slash** (*Gran corte*; 0.75 s charge): **2 masks**. Its aerial hitbox is **much bigger
  than it looks** → stay on the ground.
- **Dash Slash** (*Corte con embestida*): he jumps to a corner, charges for 0.75 s and crosses
  75% of the arena in 0.33 s.
- **Cyclone Slash** (*Corte ciclónico*): he jumps, charges in the air and comes down spinning
  towards you for ~2 s.
- **Sprint** if you're far away.

Phase 2 — **Frenzy**, his only attack: he launches himself across the arena 2–5 times and then
spins with his needle; he repeats it non-stop. Tactic: stay **in the centre**, wait for him to
**slow down**, get out of his reach and turn to hit him (nail, Shade Soul, *Sharp Shadow*).

Attack windows between 0.5 and 2 s depending on the chaining. *Sprintmaster* lets you run away
from almost everything and **save the Shade Cloak for emergencies**; *Sharp Shadow* lets you
pass through even the Cyclone Slash. Damage trick: a hit right before he releases the Cyclone and
then **3 Abyss Shrieks** (4 with *Spell Twister*) ≈ phase 1 wiped out.
Healing with no charms: during the stagger and in the phase transition (2 masks; 3 with *Quick Focus*).

## Pure Vessel · *Vasija Pura* — the Pantheon of the Knight's finale
**Health** 1600 · **At** 1600 · **AsRa** 1850 · **Stagger** 12 (9 in a combo, <1 s)
**ALL its attacks deal 2 masks.**

Phase 1:
- **Soul Daggers** (*Dagas de alma*): it moves to one side, raises its hand and throws **7
  daggers** in a fan, from the lowest to the vertical. **There are telegraphed lines** marking the gaps.
- **Triple Slash** (*Tajo triple*): three slashes advancing a third of the arena; **they reach
  you even if you're not right in front**.
- **Soul Pillars** (*Pilares de alma*): it jumps or teleports above you and crashes down; evenly
  spaced pillars come out across the whole arena, with gaps between them.
- **Lunge** (*Estocada*): covers ~50% of the arena.
- **Parry** (*Parada*; 0.5 s): if you hit it after the glow, it takes no damage and answers by
  advancing and slashing.
- **Jump** (*Salto*): if you stay in the air too long, it jumps towards you.
- **Teleport** (*Teletransporte*) and **Backstep** (*paso atrás*).

Phase 2 (below 66% health) adds:
- **Focus** (*Concentración*): it focuses like you, **doesn't heal**, surrounds itself with an
  aura that bursts; then **6 small auras** appear around the arena and explode in the order they
  came out. It chains another attack with the last explosion.

Phase 3 (below 33%) adds:
- **Void Tendrils** (*Zarcillos del Vacío*): it opens its cloak, ~0.75 s of warning, and **two big
  tendrils** sweep 60% of the arena for ~0.75 s.

How to play it:
- Triple Slash and Lunge: jump (with a pogo or a double jump for the second slash), pass through
  with the Shade Cloak, or Descending Dark. On reaching the other side there's usually an opening.
- Pillars: the first thing is to get out; you can **pogo on it** to place yourself between
  pillars, and there you have time to hit it. If you get that gap right next to it, **Abyss
  Shriek**. If you hit it from the side, use **only upward strikes** so you don't get pushed
  into a pillar.
- Parry: **don't attack**. If you trigger it, Shade Cloak through it. Descending Dark loses the
  first impact but lands the second.
- Focus: either Shade Soul from afar, or quick hits before the explosion, or Descending Dark to
  ignore it.
- Tendrils: get out of range, above it or **behind** it (behind you're completely free).
- Abyss Shriek is only viable during the stagger… and careful, it can come out of the stagger
  straight into a Parry and block it.
- Healing: the safe one is the stagger (1–2 masks with no charms, 3 with *Quick Focus*). There's
  also room during the Pillars (starting before they form), the Focus, the Daggers if you're
  already well placed, a predicted Parry and out of the Tendrils' range. *Shape of Unn + Quick
  Focus* turns all those openings into safe heals.
- The **Dream Nail doesn't work**; *Flukenest* only during the Pillars; the area effects
  (*Spore Shroom*, *Defender's Crest*) don't touch it in its Parry stance.
- It's so fast that **relying on the Shade Cloak fails**: learn to dodge without it and save it
  for the Parry.

## Sisters of Battle · *Hermanas de Batalla* — Pantheon of Hallownest room 17
**Health** 500 (phase 1) + 750×3 (phase 2) = **2750** · **At** 500 / 750 each · **AsRa** 600 / 950 each

Phase 1 identical to the Mantis Lords. In phase 2 **all three** come in:
- **Dash & Drop** (*Embestida y caída combinadas*): two sisters follow the pattern of the Lords'
  phase 2 and the third that of phase 1, **offset just after**. If the two dash, the third
  **always** does a Lance Drop. The Drops can chain all three in a row.
- **Boomerang** (*Bumerán*) with new patterns: a wide arc that meets in the centre at floor
  level; a short arc that hangs in the centre; **one wide and one short** (they meet at the ends,
  not in the centre); a single sister throwing followed by a Drop; and that pattern repeated by
  the other sister on the opposite wall.

With two alive it goes back to the Lords' phase 2 pattern but **keeps the single-sister
boomerangs**. With one alive, phase 1 again.

There are barely any openings to heal except the phase transition → *Quick Focus + Shape of
Unn*, or simply **lifeblood** (*Joni's Blessing*). Jumping and striking downwards as they pass
works to damage and reposition. A well-placed *Dreamshield* **absorbs the boomerangs and leaves
the two throwers stopped** for a few moments.

## Absolute Radiance · *Absoluto Destello* — the Pantheon of Hallownest's finale
**Health** 400 + 450 + 300 + 750 + 281 = **2181** (phases 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6)
Staggers by damage: the phase 3→4 transition (at 1150 damage) and the final blow (2181).

The same attacks as the Radiance but sped up:
- **Beam Burst** (*Ráfaga de rayos*): almost instant volleys, one after another.
- **Sword Burst** (*Ráfaga de espadas*): **two** volleys, the second rotated relative to the first.
- **Sword Rain** (*Lluvia de espadas*): they fall faster and with a shorter interval in phases
  1–2; the same speed but somewhat more often in phase 3.
- **Sword Wall** (*Muro de espadas*): faster but only **3 volleys** in phases 1–2.
- **Wall of Light** (*Muro de luz*): faster.
- **Orb** (*Orbes*): **twice as fast**, they turn much tighter to chase you, one every second,
  3–4 in total.
- **Spike Floor** (*Suelo de pinchos*): every 6–8 s in phase 2, **constant** in phase 3.
- **Big Beam** (*Gran rayo*): somewhat thinner, dangerous at 0.5 s, one every second, chained.
- **Orb Barrage** (*Bombardeo de orbes*; new, only **phase 6**): slow orbs every 2 s non-stop;
  they behave like the Radiance's and disappear if you take them off screen.

In phase 4 it can appear **over any platform**.
