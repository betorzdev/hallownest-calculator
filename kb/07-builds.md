# Combat builds

A maximum of **11 notches**. You can equip a charm that exceeds them if you have **at least one
free**: you become **overcharmed** (damage taken ×2) and can't equip anything more.
Overcharming almost never pays off in combat: the ×2 kills whole builds. It only makes sense for
fitting in *Void Heart* (0 notches, it doesn't count) or for relaxed farming.

## 1. The five decisions

Every combat build in Hollow Knight comes down to sharing 11 notches among five things:

1. **Damage per hit** — Strength (3), Fury (2).
2. **Hits per second** — Quick Slash (3). It multiplies damage **and** soul generation.
3. **Reach / safety** — Mark of Pride (3), Longnail (2), Steady Body (1), Sharp Shadow (2).
4. **Sustaining the fight** — Quick Focus (3), Shape of Unn (2), Deep Focus (4), Soul Catcher (2)
   / Soul Eater (4), Grubsong (1).
5. **Damage that doesn't require being close** — Shaman Stone (3), Spell Twister (2), the
   companions (Weaversong 2, Glowing Womb 2, Grimmchild 2).

Rule of thumb: **Quick Slash before Strength** if only one fits. Quick Slash is a **×1.47** to
DPS *and* to soul per second; Strength is a ×1.5 to the nail's damage, doesn't touch soul and
**doesn't affect the arts**. Together they're the core of almost every nail build.

## 2. Builds

### Pure nail — 11 notches
`Quick Slash (3) · Fragile/Unbreakable Strength (3) · Mark of Pride (3) · Steady Body (1) · Nailmaster's Glory (1)`

32 damage every ~0.28 s ≈ **114/s**, +25% reach and no recoil of your own, with arts charging in
0.75 s. The highest sustained-damage build in the game. No safety net: if you can't dodge the
fight, it isn't for you.
*Cheap variant*: swap Mark of Pride for Longnail (2) and add **Grubsong** (1).

### Spells — 11 notches
`Shaman Stone (3) · Spell Twister (2) · Soul Catcher (2) · Soul Eater (4)`

Abyss Shriek for **120** every 24 soul, and 22 soul per nail hit. It shines against **big targets
that don't recoil**: the Radiance, Uumuu, Flukemarm, Oblobbles, Crystal Guardian.
*Variant without Soul Eater*: remove Soul Eater (4) and add `Quick Slash (3) + Glory (1)`, which
is also more soul per second.

### Survivor / pantheons — 11 notches
`Quick Focus (3) · Shape of Unn (2) · Quick Slash (3) · Grubsong (1) · Stalwart Shell (2)`

The default build for the Pantheon of Hallownest. You heal while moving and at 0.597 s per mask,
you recover soul when you're hit and you get 1.75 s of invulnerability per hit.
*2-masks-per-heal variant*: `Quick Focus (3) + Deep Focus (4)` — 2 masks in 0.985 s, only 10%
slower than a normal mask. Excellent against the Radiance and Pure Vessel, where the openings
are short but exist.

### Minion / hands-free — 11 notches
`Weaversong (2) · Glowing Womb (2) · Grubsong (1) · Defender's Crest (1) · Sprintmaster (1) · Grimmchild (2)` (2 left free)

The companions do the damage and you just dodge. *Grubsong* feeds *Glowing Womb* with soul;
*Sprintmaster* gives the weaverlings reach and speed; *Defender's Crest* clears projectiles and
minions. It's the build the wiki recommends against **Nightmare King Grimm** and the basis of
the wall *cheese* against **Failed Champion**, **Lost Kin**, **Traitor Lord** and **God Tamer**.

### At one mask (glass cannon) — 11 notches
`Fury of the Fallen (2) · Strength (3) · Quick Slash (3) · Grubberfly's Elegy (3)`

**56 damage every ~0.28 s ≈ 200/s**, and on top of that **with projectiles**: Elegy normally
only fires at full health, but **with Fury of the Fallen equipped it also fires at 1 mask** (24
per projectile with the Pure Nail + Fury + Strength). It's the damage peak of the game, at the
cost of being one hit from death.

It's the reference build for **Grey Prince Zote** and, in general, for dream bosses: losing
doesn't kill you, so *Fragile Strength* and *Fragile Heart* **don't break**.

Watch out for two details:
- **Gaining lifeblood while at 1 mask cancels Elegy** (not Fury). *Hiveblood* brings it back
  when it regenerates the mask; with *Joni's Blessing* none of this works.
- Elegy only counts **normal masks** as full health. That's why, in the *defensive* version,
  **Lifeblood Heart/Core** lets you take hits without losing the projectiles.

### Anti-minion / swarm fights — 11 notches
`Defender's Crest (1) · Thorns of Agony (1) · Spore Shroom (1) · Quick Slash (3) · Quick Focus (3) · Grubsong (1)` (1 free)

For **Lost Kin** (Infected Balloons non-stop), **Broken Vessel**, **the Collector**,
**Flukemarm** and **Grey Prince Zote**. The area effects kill whatever comes close while you heal.

### Bindings (Nail + Shell + Soul) — 11 notches
`Strength (3) · Quick Slash (3) · Mark of Pride (3) · Nailmaster's Glory (1) · Steady Body (1)`

With the Nail Binding, the base damage is fixed at 13 and Strength raises it to **20**: it's the
only multiplier that still works. With the Soul Binding, the soul charms are worth nothing and
healing is single-use — everything goes into killing fast.
If you also wear the Charms Binding, there's no build: it's pure nail at 13 (4/7/10/13/13) and
perfect dodging.

## 3. What to bring against what

| Situation | What really changes the fight |
|---|---|
| A fast boss with short openings | **Quick Focus + Shape of Unn** |
| A big boss that doesn't recoil | **Shaman Stone + Abyss Shriek** (4 impacts) |
| A light boss you push away | **Steady Body**, and upward strikes |
| A boss that summons minions | **Defender's Crest**, **Thorns of Agony**, **Cyclone Slash** |
| A boss that gives no soul (False Knight, the Collector) | **Grubsong**, **Dream Wielder**, **Weaversong + Grubsong** |
| A boss with blockable projectiles | **Dreamshield** (mantis boomerangs, Grimm's bats, Winged Nosk's blobs) |
| Attacks impossible to dodge (Traitor Lord's Ground Pound, Wall of Light) | **Shade Cloak** or **Descending Dark** (i-frames) |
| A boss that pushes you into the spikes | **Steady Body** + upward strikes |
| Many fights in a row with no bench | **Hiveblood**, **Lifeblood Heart/Core** |
| You need reach without getting close | **Grubberfly's Elegy** (at full health) |
| A dream boss (you don't really die) | **Fragile Strength** and **Fragile Heart** for free |

## 4. Costly mistakes

- **Wearing Strength without Quick Slash** if both fit: the right order for buying notches is
  Quick Slash → Strength → reach.
- **Overcharming to fit one more charm**: ×2 damage taken turns any 2-mask fight into a 4-mask one.
- **Quick Slash with horizontal strikes against light bosses**: you chase them all over the
  arena. Strike upwards, or add *Steady Body*.
- **Chaining Desolate Dive too fast**: if you cast it before the previous i-frames expire,
  **you cancel the new ones**.
- **Attacking a boss in a Parry stance** (the Hollow Knight, Pure Vessel, Hornet Sentinel,
  Oro/Mato in Barricade): you deal no damage and eat the counter.
- **Hitting a staggered boss when you meant to heal**: the hit **ends the stagger**. The only
  useful exception is Failed Champion's armour, which extends it.
- **Counting stagger by damage**: it goes by **number of hits**. An Old Nail staggers as fast as
  a Pure one (and *Heavy Blow* takes off 1 hit).
- **Joni's Blessing with a healing build**: with Joni's Blessing **you can't heal**, full stop.
